* Overview
My Doom Emacs configuration is currently quite simplistic, with small changes managed through config.org and longer or repetitive configuration separated into the elisp/ directory for clarity and organization.

I use Org-roam to implement the Zettelkasten method, helping me learn efficiently and keep my notes well-structured.

This configuration is heavily inspired by users like =sunnyhasija= and =tecosaur=. Setting it up would have been much harder without their excellent guides on writing accessible and effective Emacs configurations.

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp :tangle "config.el"
;;; $DOOMDIR/config.el -*- lexical-binding: t; -*-
#+end_src

** theme:
#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp :tangle "config.el"
(setq doom-theme 'doom-nord)
#+end_src

** frame settings:
#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp :tangle "config.el"
(setq frame '((width . 203)
              (height . 60)
              (top . 20)
              (left . 0)))
(setq initial-frame-alist frame
      default-frame-alist frame)
#+end_src

** org-roam:
#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp :tangle "config.el"
(defvar klippel/org-roam-templates
   '(("d" "default" plain
          "%?"
          :if-new (file+head "%<%Y%m%d%H%M%S>-${slug}.org" "#+title: ${title}\n")
          :unnarrowed t)

         ("f" "French" plain
          "%?"
          :if-new (file+head "%<%Y%m%d%H%M%S>-${slug}.org" "#+title: ${title}\n#+filetags: :large_topic:\n")
          :unnarrowed t)

         ("c" "C standard library" plain
          "* Description:\n %? \n\n* Defining exemple:\n\n #+begin_src %^{Language}\n\n#+end_src"
          :if-new (file+head "%<%Y%m%d%H%M%S>-${slug}.org" "#+title: ${title}\n#+filetags: :standard_library:\n")
          :unnarrowed t)

         ("l" "LeetCode Exercise" plain
          "* Micro-skills used:\n - %?\n\n* Language used:\n \n\n* Solution\n\n#+begin_src %^{Language}\n\n#+end_src\n\n* Reference:\n\n- \n- [[roam:LeetCode Exercise]]"
          :if-new (file+head "%<%Y%m%d%H%M%S>-${slug}.org" "#+title: ${title}\n#+filetags: :leetCode_exercise:\n")
          :unnarrowed t)))

(setq org-roam-capture-templates klippel/org-roam-templates)
#+end_src
